CHEST PAIN
Chest pain with shortness of breath may indicate a heart attack or another serious cardiac condition. Immediate emergency medical evaluation is recommended.
SOURCE: American Heart Association

------------------------------------------------

STROKE
Facial drooping, slurred speech, or limb weakness are warning signs of stroke. Seek emergency medical care immediately.
SOURCE: American Stroke Association

------------------------------------------------

LOW OXYGEN
Oxygen saturation below 90% requires urgent medical evaluation and oxygen therapy if necessary.
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)

------------------------------------------------

HIGH FEVER
High fever accompanied by confusion or seizures requires immediate medical assessment.
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)

------------------------------------------------

ABDOMINAL PAIN
Persistent severe abdominal pain with vomiting or blood in the stool requires urgent medical evaluation.
SOURCE: NICE Guideline

------------------------------------------------

DIABETES
Patients with diabetes experiencing confusion, dizziness, or persistent vomiting should seek medical evaluation promptly.
SOURCE: American Diabetes Association

------------------------------------------------

SEVERE BLEEDING
Severe bleeding requires immediate first aid and emergency medical attention. Apply direct pressure to the wound until help arrives.
SOURCE: Red Cross First Aid Guidelines

------------------------------------------------

PREGNANCY BLEEDING
Bleeding during pregnancy should always be evaluated immediately by a healthcare professional.
SOURCE: ACOG

------------------------------------------------

ASTHMA ATTACK
Asthma with wheezing and shortness of breath requires use of a rescue inhaler and immediate medical attention if symptoms persist.
SOURCE: Global Initiative for Asthma (GINA)

------------------------------------------------

COPD EXACERBATION
Patients with COPD experiencing worsening breathlessness should receive prompt medical evaluation.
SOURCE: GOLD COPD Guidelines

------------------------------------------------

HEART DISEASE
Patients with known heart disease who develop chest pain or breathlessness should seek emergency care immediately.
SOURCE: American College of Cardiology

------------------------------------------------

SEIZURE
A seizure or sudden loss of consciousness requires immediate emergency evaluation to identify the underlying cause.
SOURCE: Epilepsy Foundation

------------------------------------------------

HEAD INJURY
Loss of consciousness, repeated vomiting, or confusion following a head injury requires emergency medical assessment.
SOURCE: CDC Head Injury Guideline

------------------------------------------------

SEVERE HEADACHE
A sudden severe headache accompanied by neurological symptoms may indicate a stroke or brain hemorrhage and requires emergency care.
SOURCE: American Academy of Neurology

------------------------------------------------

ALLERGIC REACTION
Swollen tongue or swollen throat may indicate a severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis). Seek emergency medical treatment immediately.
SOURCE: World Allergy Organization

------------------------------------------------

BURNS
Large, deep, or electrical burns require immediate emergency medical evaluation.
SOURCE: American Burn Association

------------------------------------------------

FRACTURE
A suspected fracture with severe pain, swelling, or deformity should be immobilized and evaluated promptly.
SOURCE: American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons

------------------------------------------------

KIDNEY DISEASE
Patients with kidney disease who develop severe swelling, breathing difficulty, or confusion require urgent medical evaluation.
SOURCE: National Kidney Foundation

------------------------------------------------

SUICIDAL THOUGHTS
Any suicidal thoughts require immediate assessment by a mental health professional or emergency services.
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)

------------------------------------------------

RESPIRATORY INFECTION
Patients with fever, cough, sore throat, and difficulty breathing should receive medical evaluation, especially if symptoms worsen or oxygen levels decrease.
SOURCE: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

------------------------------------------------

DEHYDRATION
Persistent vomiting, diarrhea, dizziness, or signs of dehydration require increased fluid intake and prompt medical evaluation if symptoms are severe or persistent.
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)

------------------------------------------------

FOOD POISONING
Persistent vomiting, diarrhea, severe abdominal pain, or signs of dehydration after eating contaminated food should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.
SOURCE: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

------------------------------------------------

PNEUMONIA
Persistent fever, cough, shortness of breath, or chest discomfort may require evaluation for a lower respiratory infection.
SOURCE: American Lung Association

------------------------------------------------

INFLUENZA
Fever, cough, sore throat, and body aches lasting several days should be medically evaluated, especially in older adults or individuals with chronic illness.
SOURCE: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

------------------------------------------------

COVID-19
Patients with fever, cough, shortness of breath, or low oxygen levels should receive appropriate medical evaluation and follow current public health recommendations.
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)

------------------------------------------------

BRONCHITIS
Persistent cough with wheezing or breathing difficulty should be evaluated by a healthcare professional, especially if symptoms worsen.
SOURCE: American Lung Association

------------------------------------------------

HYPERTENSIVE EMERGENCY
Very high blood pressure accompanied by severe headache, chest pain, confusion, or neurological symptoms requires emergency medical evaluation.
SOURCE: American Heart Association

------------------------------------------------

PALPITATIONS
Rapid or irregular heartbeat associated with dizziness, chest pain, or fainting requires prompt medical assessment.
SOURCE: American College of Cardiology

------------------------------------------------

FAINTING
Sudden loss of consciousness should be medically evaluated to determine the underlying cause, particularly if associated with chest pain or neurological symptoms.
SOURCE: Mayo Clinic

------------------------------------------------

MIGRAINE
A severe headache with nausea or vomiting should be medically evaluated, particularly if it is the first occurrence or significantly different from previous headaches.
SOURCE: American Migraine Foundation

------------------------------------------------

VERTIGO
Persistent dizziness affecting balance or accompanied by neurological symptoms requires medical assessment.
SOURCE: National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders

------------------------------------------------

CONSTIPATION
Persistent abdominal discomfort with prolonged constipation or vomiting should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.
SOURCE: NICE Guideline

------------------------------------------------

CELLULITIS
A rapidly spreading skin rash with swelling, pain, or fever requires prompt medical evaluation.
SOURCE: Infectious Diseases Society of America

------------------------------------------------

INSECT BITE
An insect bite causing severe swelling, difficulty breathing, or widespread rash requires immediate medical attention.
SOURCE: American Academy of Allergy, Asthma & Immunology

------------------------------------------------

ANIMAL BITE
Animal bites should be cleaned immediately and evaluated by a healthcare professional because of the risk of infection or rabies exposure.
SOURCE: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

------------------------------------------------

EYE INJURY
Sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, or chemical exposure to the eye requires immediate emergency medical evaluation.
SOURCE: American Academy of Ophthalmology

------------------------------------------------

DENTAL EMERGENCY
Severe dental pain with facial swelling, bleeding, or fever requires prompt dental or medical evaluation.
SOURCE: American Dental Association

------------------------------------------------

HEAT STROKE
High body temperature, confusion, dizziness, or loss of consciousness after heat exposure requires immediate emergency treatment.
SOURCE: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

------------------------------------------------

HYPOTHERMIA
Low body temperature accompanied by confusion, excessive shivering, or loss of consciousness requires emergency medical evaluation.
SOURCE: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

------------------------------------------------

PANIC ATTACK
Sudden chest tightness, rapid heartbeat, dizziness, or shortness of breath should be medically evaluated to rule out serious physical conditions before considering anxiety-related causes.
SOURCE: National Institute of Mental Health
